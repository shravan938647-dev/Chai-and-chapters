Our Story & Philosophy

Where Slow Sips Meet
Meaningful Stories

Founded in 2024 by Aarav Sharma, Chai & Chapters Café was born out of a simple longing: a quiet, welcoming sanctuary amidst the bustling heart of C-Scheme, Jaipur where you can simply pause and unwind.

Whether you are a university student preparing for finals, a young professional seeking a work desk with stable Wi-Fi and power outlets, a family enjoying a relaxed weekend snack, or a solo bookworm diving into vintage poetry—we have built every corner with you in mind.
